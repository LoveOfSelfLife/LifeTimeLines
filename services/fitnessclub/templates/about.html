About Our Club

Active Friends Club is a private club located in Cranford, New Jersey, designed to provide an affordable and community-oriented fitness experience. The club provides the tools, structure & community to encourage and motivate members to remain active and achieve their personal and long-lasting fitness goals.

Active Friends Club provides a supportive community environment where fitness becomes a shared journey, thus giving members a lower cost alternative to traditional private gym memberships.

Mission

To create a welcoming community where members support each other in achieving their fitness goals through fitness technology, structured programs, social connections, all while using affordable quality facilities.

Why?

Community Focused

Exercising is more enjoyable and motivating when done with friends. Active Friends Club makes it easy to coordinate gym visits with fellow members.

Affordable

Achieve your fitness goals while using affordable quality fitness facilities.

Supportive

A structured and dcommunity-based approach to fitness provides the motivation and accountability you need to achieve your long-term health goals.

Location & Access

Serving the Cranford, New Jersey area

Our club operates in Cranford, utilizing a locally accessible and affordable quality fitness facility.
